# What's New

The October 2026 network upgrade (version 8.2) is mostly about communities: more ways to reward the people who hold a token, and more ways for a project to make its token its own. Here's what it brings you, with links to the guides.

## Any token can be stakable, for good

Every token on BeeZee can now have a **compounded reward**: stake the token itself and share the prizes paid out to its stakers. There's one per token, it has no end date, and it can pay **several prize tokens at once**. Prizes come from anyone, not only the token's team: a fan, a partner project or another community can all add one.

* **Daily prizes** pay a set amount every day for as many days as they're funded. Days with nobody staking are skipped, so no prize is wasted.
* **Airdrops to stakers** split an amount between everyone staking right now, in one go.
* **Claim everything at once**: one transaction collects all your prizes, in every prize token.

In the Communities app, a token's compounded reward is its **Holders pool**.

## Reach another community

A project can now gift its token to the people who stake a *different* token, once or every day. Those stakers find the token in their wallet and on that token's Communities page, so it's a way to get in front of an audience that is already active. You don't need to own the other token or ask its creator.

## Give your token its own look

A token's admin can attach a **brand kit** to it: a font and a set of colours, with separate palettes for light and dark mode. It's stored with the token on the network, so every app that supports brand kits picks it up. The Communities app already draws each token's page and card in its own colours.

## Fix your token's supply for good

A token's admin can now **renounce admin**. Once they do, nobody can ever mint more of the token or change it again, and everyone sees **Fixed supply**. It's permanent, so it's never a launch step: some projects do it later, to show their holders the supply can't grow.

## Tidier reward lists

Once a simple reward has finished and everyone has left it, anyone can **Delete** it from Token Factory's **Rewards** directory. Nothing is paid out and nothing is lost; the lists just stay focused on rewards you can still join.

{% hint style="success" %}
**For example.** The book club makes **$PAGE** stakable, adds a daily $PAGE prize and gives it a warm paper-and-ink brand kit. A gaming guild then adds 30 days of **$GUILD** to the $PAGE holders pool. Book club members now earn two tokens for staking one, on a page that looks like the club's own.
{% endhint %}
