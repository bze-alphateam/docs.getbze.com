# Features

BeeZee brings together everything you need to use, create, and grow — all in one place. Here is what you can do, in plain terms.

1. [**Stake BZE**](stake-bze.md) — earn a share of the rewards, including inflation, and help secure the network.
2. [**Trading**](trading.md) — swap through liquidity pools or trade on order-book markets.
3. [**Create Your Own Token**](creating-tokens.md) — launch a token and reward the people who support it.
4. [**Earn Rewards**](earning-rewards.md) — join staking programs across the ecosystem to earn more.
5. [**Burning Tokens**](token-burning.md) — take part in community burns and burning raffles.
6. [**Decentralized Content Sharing**](decentralized-content-sharing.md) — publish and discover content stored on-chain.
7. [**Cross-Chain Transfers**](cross-chain-transfers.md) — move your assets between connected blockchains.
8. [**Building Communities**](building-communities.md) — everything a project needs to run its own token.
9. [**Any Token as a Fee Token**](fee-token.md) — let users pay fees in your token, not BZE.
10. [**Simple Solutions**](simple-solutions.md) — ready-made apps you can make your own.

## More on the way

BeeZee is actively developed, and new capabilities are added regularly. See the up-to-date [roadmap on the website](https://getbze.com/#roadmap).
