# Features

BeeZee brings together everything you need to use, create, and grow — all in one place. Here is what you can do, in plain terms.

* [**Create Your Own Token**](creating-tokens.md) — launch a token in seconds, no coding required.
* [**Trade on the DEX**](trading.md) — buy, sell and list assets on a decentralized exchange.
* [**Earn Rewards**](earning-rewards.md) — put your coins to work through staking and rewards.
* [**Token Burning**](token-burning.md) — take part in regular, community-driven coin burns.
* [**Decentralized News**](decentralized-news.md) — publish and read content stored on-chain.
* [**Cross-Chain Transfers**](cross-chain-transfers.md) — move your assets between blockchains.

## More on the way

BeeZee is actively developed, and new capabilities are added regularly — such as liquidity-pool swaps and extra tools for token creators. See the up-to-date [roadmap on the website](https://getbze.com/#roadmap).
