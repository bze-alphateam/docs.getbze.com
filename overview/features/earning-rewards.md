# Earn Rewards

There is more than one way to put your tokens to work on BeeZee. Each one has its own guide; this page is the map.

## Stake your BZE

Back a validator — one of the computers that keep the network running — and earn a share of the rewards the network produces. Your BZE stays yours the whole time; you can claim rewards, move your stake or unstake whenever you like (unstaking comes with a waiting period). It's done in the **Staking** app, and the DEX's **Staking** page offers it too.

{% content-ref url="../../apps/staking/staking-your-bze.md" %}
[staking-your-bze.md](../../apps/staking/staking-your-bze.md)
{% endcontent-ref %}

## Stake a community's token in its holders pool

Many tokens have a **holders pool**: stake the token itself and share the daily prizes that anyone can add to it. There's no end date, and you'll find it on the token's page in the **Communities** app.

{% content-ref url="../../apps/communities/holders-pool.md" %}
[holders-pool.md](../../apps/communities/holders-pool.md)
{% endcontent-ref %}

## Join a fixed-term staking program

A **staking program** runs for a set number of days: stake the token it asks for and earn a daily prize while it lasts. Some programs reward a community's own token, others pay you in that token for staking a different coin. Browse them on a token's page in the **Communities** app, or on the DEX's **Staking** page.

{% content-ref url="../../apps/communities/staking-programs.md" %}
[staking-programs.md](../../apps/communities/staking-programs.md)
{% endcontent-ref %}

## Provide liquidity, then boost it

Add two tokens to a liquidity pool on the DEX and you earn a slice of the fee from every swap through it. Some pools also offer **Boost Rewards**: lock the LP tokens you received into a reward program and earn extra rewards on top of your trading fees.

{% content-ref url="../../apps/dex/liquidity-pools.md" %}
[liquidity-pools.md](../../apps/dex/liquidity-pools.md)
{% endcontent-ref %}

## Everything in one place on the DEX

The DEX's **Staking** page brings it together: your BZE staking, the staking programs, and the holder rewards in its **Denom rewards** section, with your own positions and pending rewards up front.

{% content-ref url="../../apps/dex/staking-and-earning.md" %}
[staking-and-earning.md](../../apps/dex/staking-and-earning.md)
{% endcontent-ref %}

{% hint style="success" %}
**For example.** Say you are holding **$PAGE** from a book club and **$GUILD** from a gaming guild. You stake your $PAGE in its holders pool, join a 30-day program that pays $GUILD for staking the $GUILD–BZE LP token, and keep some BZE staked with a validator. Three kinds of rewards, each claimed whenever you like.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Rewards are estimates, not promises.** The rates the apps show depend on how much is staked and how many prizes are running right now, so they move over time. Unstaking also comes with a waiting period, shown before you confirm.
{% endhint %}
