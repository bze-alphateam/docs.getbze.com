# Earn Rewards

There is more than one way to put your tokens to work on BeeZee. Each one has its own guide; this page is the map.

## Stake your BZE

Back a validator — one of the computers that keep the network running — and earn a share of the rewards the network produces. Your BZE stays yours the whole time; you can claim rewards, move your stake or unstake whenever you like (unstaking comes with a waiting period). It's done in the **Staking** app, and the DEX's **Staking** page offers it too.

{% content-ref url="../../apps/staking/staking-your-bze.md" %}
[staking-your-bze.md](../../apps/staking/staking-your-bze.md)
{% endcontent-ref %}

## Stake a community's token in its compounded reward

Many tokens have a **compounded reward**: stake the token itself and share the prizes that anyone can add to it, in several tokens, every day or as airdrops. There's one per token and no end date. You'll find it on the DEX's **Staking** page, and on the token's page in the **Communities** app, where it's called the **Holders pool**.

{% content-ref url="../../apps/communities/holders-pool.md" %}
[holders-pool.md](../../apps/communities/holders-pool.md)
{% endcontent-ref %}

## Join a simple reward

A **simple reward** runs for a set number of days: stake the token it asks for and earn a daily amount while it lasts. Some reward a community's own token, others pay you in that token for staking a different coin. Browse them on the DEX's **Staking** page, or on a token's page in the **Communities** app, where they're called **Fixed-term programs**.

{% content-ref url="../../apps/communities/staking-programs.md" %}
[staking-programs.md](../../apps/communities/staking-programs.md)
{% endcontent-ref %}

## Provide liquidity, then boost it

Add two tokens to a liquidity pool on the DEX and you earn a slice of the fee from every swap through it. Some pools also offer **Boost Rewards**: lock the LP tokens you received into a simple reward and earn extra rewards on top of your trading fees.

{% content-ref url="../../apps/dex/liquidity-pools.md" %}
[liquidity-pools.md](../../apps/dex/liquidity-pools.md)
{% endcontent-ref %}

## Everything in one place on the DEX

The DEX's **Staking** page brings it together: your BZE staking, its **Simple rewards** and its **Compounded rewards**, with your own positions and pending rewards up front.

{% content-ref url="../../apps/dex/staking-and-earning.md" %}
[staking-and-earning.md](../../apps/dex/staking-and-earning.md)
{% endcontent-ref %}

{% hint style="success" %}
**For example.** Say you are holding **$PAGE** from a book club and **$GUILD** from a gaming guild. You stake your $PAGE in its compounded reward, join a 30-day simple reward that pays $GUILD for staking the $GUILD–BZE LP token, and keep some BZE staked with a validator. Three kinds of rewards, each claimed whenever you like.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Rewards are estimates, not promises.** The rates the apps show depend on how much is staked and how many prizes are running right now, so they move over time. Unstaking also comes with a waiting period, shown before you confirm.
{% endhint %}
