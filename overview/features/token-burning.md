# Burning Tokens

BeeZee lets anyone permanently remove coins from circulation, and keeps doing it on its own in the background.

* **Automatic, regular burns.** Part of the fees collected across the ecosystem is sent to a shared burning pot. Every few weeks the network burns everything in the pot automatically, with nobody needing to vote or press a button. How often is set by the network, currently every 4 weeks.
* **Burn what you hold.** Anyone can add coins to the pot: BZE, any community token, and even bridged tokens, which are swapped to BZE and burned. Liquidity pool tokens are locked forever instead, which takes them out of circulation just the same.
* **Everything in the open.** Every burn is recorded on the network, and the Burner app shows the countdown to the next burn, the total burned and each coin's own burn history.
* **Burning raffles.** Contribute to a coin's raffle for a chance to win a share of its pot; whatever is left in the pot when the raffle ends is burned.

{% hint style="success" %}
**For example.** The book club wants to show members that **$PAGE** is getting scarcer over time. It burns a batch of unused $PAGE from its treasury, and members can follow it on the $PAGE page in the Burner app: waiting in the pot, then burned on the next scheduled burn and added to the all-time total.
{% endhint %}
