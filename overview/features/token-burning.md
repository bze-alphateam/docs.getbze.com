# Burning Tokens

BeeZee supports burning tokens to permanently reduce supply. The community runs regular, governance-approved burns, and part of the fees collected across the ecosystem is sent to be burned over time. Anyone can also join **burning raffles** for a chance to earn a share of the amount being burned — adding a bit of fun to the process.
