# Token Burning

BeeZee is a community committed to reducing its coin supply over time through regular, governance-approved burns. Fees collected across the ecosystem are sent to be burned, and the community can take part in burning raffles for a chance to earn a share of the burned amount — adding a bit of fun to the process.
