# Building Communities

Everything above comes together for the people building projects on BeeZee. A community can have its own token and run it like an established project would — all without writing code:

* **Launch a token** for your project or community.
* **Give it a home**: a page in the Communities app, in your token's colours and font, that works as your project's own website.
* **Reward your holders** with a compounded reward, simple rewards and airdrops.
* **Engage your community** with burns: anyone can burn your token from its page, and you can start a burning raffle for it in Token Factory, which then shows up on the token's page for your members to join.
* **Grow over time** as new capabilities are added to the network.

In short, everything the BeeZee community does for BZE, your community can do for its own token.

{% hint style="success" %}
**For example.** A gaming guild launches **$GUILD**, gives it a brand kit, rewards the members who stake it in its compounded reward, and adds a $GUILD–BZE pool so fees are paid in $GUILD. It shares its Communities page as the guild's website, and when it starts a burning raffle for $GUILD, members find the raffle card right on that page. The guild never wrote a line of blockchain code — and to its members, it feels like they have a network of their own.
{% endhint %}
