# Building Communities

Everything above comes together for the people building projects on BeeZee. A community can have its own token and run it like an established project would — all without writing code:

* **Launch a token** for your project or community.
* **Reward your holders** with staking programs and airdrops.
* **Engage your community** with burning raffles and other incentives.
* **Grow over time** as new capabilities are added to the network.

In short, everything the BeeZee community does for BZE, your community can do for its own token.

{% hint style="success" %}
**For example.** A gaming guild launches **$GUILD**, rewards the members who stake it, runs a burning raffle for its community, and adds a $GUILD–BZE pool so fees are paid in $GUILD. The guild never wrote a line of blockchain code — and to its members, it feels like they have a network of their own.
{% endhint %}
