# Stake BZE

Staking is the simplest way to put your BZE to work. By staking, you help secure the network and, in return, you receive a share of the rewards — including the new coins created through inflation. Your coins stay yours the whole time: staking delegates them to a validator, and you can unstake whenever you like after a short unlocking period.
