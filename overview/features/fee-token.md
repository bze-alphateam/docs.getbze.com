# Any Token as a Fee Token

On BeeZee, your own token can be used to pay network fees — as long as it has a liquidity pool paired with BZE. That means the people using your app never need to hold BZE: they interact using your token, and the blockchain does its work quietly in the background.

This is what lets a project build an experience that feels entirely its own — its token, its brand, its community — with BeeZee working for them and their users behind the scenes.
