# Create Your Own Token

Create your own token in seconds — no coding required. Your token is a first-class citizen on BeeZee: it can be traded, transferred to other blockchains, and used to grow and reward a community.

As a token creator you can:

* **Set up staking programs** so people who stake your token earn rewards.
* **Airdrop your stakers** — reward everyone who stakes your token.
* **Target existing communities** — airdrop the people who already stake a particular coin, to reach an audience that is likely to care about your project.
* **Reward traders _(coming soon)_** — reward the people who trade your token in a chosen market.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Coming with the v8.2.0 network upgrade** (currently in review and reaching testnet soon): **Denom Rewards.** Today a staking program hands out a single reward; Denom Rewards let your programs offer several different reward coins at once, giving you far more flexibility in how you incentivize your holders.
{% endhint %}
