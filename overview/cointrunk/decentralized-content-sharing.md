# Decentralized Content Sharing

Decentralized Content Sharing is CoinTrunk's platform for publishing and reading content that lives on the BeeZee blockchain. Instead of a single company deciding what gets published, the community governs it: trusted publishers are elected, and the rules for what can be shared are set through governance.

The result is a transparent, censorship-resistant way to publish and discover content. Each article's record lives on-chain — its title, a link to the full piece on one of the accepted websites, and a picture link — so the list of what was published can't be quietly changed.
