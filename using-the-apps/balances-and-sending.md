# Balances & Sending

Click the **Wallet** button in any BeeZee app to open the wallet panel — your home for everything you hold.

## Your balances

The panel lists all your token balances, each with its approximate value in US dollars so you can see what everything is worth at a glance. The order is always the same: BZE comes first, then verified tokens before unverified ones, then the tokens you actually hold before the ones at zero, and finally the biggest value first. Your LP tokens from liquidity pools aren't listed here — you'll find those on the DEX's pool pages.

On a token's page in the Communities app, the wallet puts that token on top — even if you don't hold any yet — and folds the rest away. Tap **Show all balances** to see everything else, and **Hide other balances** to fold it back.

If you've used **Buy BZE** recently, a **Recent Buys** list appears above your balances. It shows each purchase and how far along it is, and tapping one opens its details. Purchases stay there for up to an hour; you can dismiss them sooner with the **×**.

## Sending tokens

Sending a coin to someone takes a few seconds:

1. **Pick a coin** from your balances.
2. **Enter an amount** — or tap **Max** to send everything you hold of it.
3. **Paste the recipient's address** (it starts with `bze…`). The app checks that it's valid before you continue.
4. **Add a note** if you like — an optional short memo that travels with the transfer.
5. **Confirm**, then approve the request in your wallet.

Once it's sent, use the **View on Explorer** link to watch it confirm on the public [block explorer](https://explorer.getbze.com/).

{% hint style="info" %}
**Double-check the address.** Transfers are final once they're confirmed on the blockchain — there's no undo button and no support desk that can reverse them. A quick look at the first and last few characters is always worth it.
{% endhint %}
