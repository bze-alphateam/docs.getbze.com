# Balances & Sending

Click the **Wallet** button in any BeeZee app to open the wallet panel — your home for everything you hold.

## Your balances

The panel lists all your token balances, each with its approximate value in US dollars so you can see what everything is worth at a glance. BZE comes first, followed by verified tokens, then everything else ordered by value. To keep things tidy, smaller and unverified balances are tucked away — tap **Show all balances** to reveal the rest.

## Sending tokens

Sending a coin to someone takes a few seconds:

1. **Pick a coin** from your balances.
2. **Enter an amount** — or tap **Max** to send everything you hold of it.
3. **Paste the recipient's address** (it starts with `bze…`). The app checks that it's valid before you continue.
4. **Add a note** if you like — an optional short memo that travels with the transfer.
5. **Confirm**, then approve the request in your wallet.

Once it's sent, use the **View on Explorer** link to watch it confirm on the public [block explorer](https://explorer.getbze.com/).

{% hint style="info" %}
**Double-check the address.** Transfers are final once they're confirmed on the blockchain — there's no undo button and no support desk that can reverse them. A quick look at the first and last few characters is always worth it.
{% endhint %}
