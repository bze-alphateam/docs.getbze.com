# Staking & Earning

The DEX isn't only for trading. Its **Staking** tab opens **Staking & Rewards**, one page for every way to put your tokens to work and earn while you hold them. At the top you'll see your **Total Value Staked**, how many **Active Stakes** you have and your **Unclaimed Rewards**, and a search box to find a token's opportunities quickly.

The page has three sections, from top to bottom: **Network staking**, **Simple rewards** and **Compounded rewards**.

## Network staking

The first section holds one card, **BZE Native Staking**: staking BZE itself. Validators are the machines that keep the BeeZee network running. When you stake (delegate) your BZE to one, you help secure the chain and earn a share of the rewards it produces, including the new BZE created by inflation. Your BZE never leaves your control: you're lending your vote of confidence, not handing it over.

The card shows the current **APR**, **Your Stake**, your **Rewards** waiting to be collected, any **Pending Unlock** (BZE on its way back to you, with the date it arrives), and the unlock period. Select the card for its actions:

* **Claim** collects your BZE staking rewards right here in the DEX.
* **Stake** and **Unstake** open the [Staking](../staking/README.md) app, which is built for choosing validators and managing your delegations.

## Simple rewards

Next come the **simple rewards**: campaigns, each with an end date, where you stake one token and share a daily amount paid in another (or the same) token. Simple rewards for LP tokens are the [Boost Rewards](liquidity-pools.md#boost-rewards) of the liquidity pools, so you'll find them here too.

Each card is tagged **Simple reward** and shows whether it is **Running**, **Finished** or **Waiting for stakers**, its estimated **APR** (a yearly rate, based on current conditions) and four numbers: **Unlock** (the waiting time after you unstake), **Daily** (how much is shared out each day), **Minimum** (the smallest stake it accepts) and **Total** (how much everyone has staked). Select a card for **Stake**, **Unstake** and **Claim**:

* **Stake** shows your **Estimated Daily Rewards** before you select **Confirm Stake**.
* **Unstake** shows an **Unstaking Notice** with how many days your tokens stay locked before they come back.
* **Claim** collects the rewards you've earned so far.

## Compounded rewards

At the bottom, **Compounded rewards** lists every token that has one: stake the token and share the prizes **anyone** adds to it, in several tokens, either as a **daily** prize or as one-off **airdrops**. There's one per token and it has no end date. The token's team, its fans or its partners can all add to the prizes. Each card is called **Stake TICKER, earn prizes**, is tagged **Compounded reward** and is labelled **Paying daily** or **No daily schedule — airdrops only**.

A card shows what it **Pays per day**, its **Total staked**, how long the **Unlock** takes (or **Instant**), and its **Minimum** stake. Once you've joined, it also shows **Your stake** with your estimated share per day, and your **Pending prizes**.

* **Stake** joins it (the button becomes **Add** once you're in). Adding more pays out your pending prizes first, in the same transaction.
* **Claim** collects your pending prizes. Your stake stays in and keeps earning.
* **Claim all compounded rewards** appears at the top of the section whenever you have prizes waiting, with how many rewards they come from in brackets, and collects them all in one transaction.
* **Exit** pays out your pending prizes, then releases your **whole** stake. Exiting is all or nothing; there's no partial exit. If it has an unlock period, your tokens show under your pending unlocks and earn nothing until it ends; with **Instant**, they're back in your wallet straight away.

Changed your mind after exiting? You can stake again whenever you like: right away with other tokens you hold, or with the returning ones once their unlock period ends.

{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
Rewards are **claimed manually**. They build up over time, and you collect them whenever you like. They don't get added to your stake automatically.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
Unstaking often isn't instant. BZE staking, simple rewards and many compounded rewards have an **unlock period**, during which your tokens are locked and earning nothing before they return to your wallet. Check the period shown on the card before you commit.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="success" %}
**For example.** A gaming guild funds a daily **$GUILD** prize in the $GUILD compounded reward. Members stake their $GUILD from the Staking page, watch their share tick up, and claim whenever they like. When a partner studio airdrops some extra $GUILD to its stakers to celebrate a launch, everyone staking gets a slice, simply for being part of the guild.
{% endhint %}
