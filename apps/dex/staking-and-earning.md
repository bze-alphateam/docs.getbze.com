# Staking & Earning

The DEX isn't only for trading — its **Staking** page lets you put your tokens to work and earn rewards while you hold them. There are two ways to do it.

* **Stake BZE with a validator.** Validators are the machines that keep the BeeZee network running. When you stake (delegate) your BZE to one, you help secure the chain and earn a share of the rewards it produces, including the new BZE created by inflation. Your BZE never leaves your control — you're lending your vote of confidence, not handing it over.
* **Join reward programs.** Some tokens run programs that pay you for staking them. Each program shows an **estimated APR** (a yearly rate, based on current conditions), your **daily rewards**, and an **unlock period** — the waiting time before your tokens come back when you unstake.

{% hint style="info" %}
Rewards are **claimed manually** — they build up over time, and you collect them whenever you like from the Staking page. They don't get added to your stake automatically.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
Unstaking isn't instant. Both BZE staking and token reward programs have an **unlock period**, during which your tokens are locked and earning nothing before they return to your wallet. Check the period shown before you commit.
{% endhint %}
