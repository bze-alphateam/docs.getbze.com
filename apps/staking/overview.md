# Overview

The Staking app is the home for securing the network and earning staking rewards. From here you can choose a validator to stake your BZE with, claim the rewards you earn, move your stake between validators, unstake when you want to, and vote on the proposals that shape the network.

Staking keeps your coins in your control the whole time: you are simply choosing a validator to back. In return, you receive a share of the network rewards, including the new coins created through inflation. The one thing to watch is your validator's reliability, because a validator that misbehaves or stays offline can be penalised, and the stake behind it with it.

Start with the first guide to pick a validator and stake, then use the second to claim rewards, unstake, and move your stake around. The third shows how your staked BZE gives you a vote on network proposals.
