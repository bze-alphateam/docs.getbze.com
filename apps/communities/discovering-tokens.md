# Discovering Tokens

The [Communities app](https://communities.getbze.com) opens on the **Token directory**: every token people have created with Token Factory, in one place. It is the front door to the projects building on BeeZee. Browse them, find one that catches your eye, and step into its world.

## Searching

Know what you're looking for? Type in the search box at the top. It matches a token's **name**, its **ticker** (like $PAGE or $GUILD), or its full **token ID**, the long code that starts with `factory/`. Results update as you type, and **Clear** empties the box again.

If nothing matches, the directory says so and offers **Clear search**, so you're one click away from the full list.

## Filtering and sorting

Under the search box, a row of filters narrows the list:

* **All**: every community token.
* **Verified**: only the tokens that carry the verified badge.
* **Staking rewards**: tokens you can stake to earn something, either in a holders pool or in a staking program.
* **Tradable**: tokens that sit in at least one liquidity pool, so you can swap into them.

Next to the search box, the sort menu sets the order:

* **Sort: Name A–Z**: alphabetical by ticker (the default).
* **Sort: Market cap**: the biggest projects first.
* **Sort: 24h volume**: the most traded tokens of the last day first.

Tokens without a price or without any trading go to the end of those last two lists. A line on the right tells you how many tokens you're looking at, for example **Showing 21–40 of 53 tokens**.

## Browsing the pages

The directory is split into numbered pages. Use **Previous**, **Next** or a page number at the bottom to move between them; on a phone you get **Prev**, **Next** and **Page X of Y**. Changing the search, a filter or the sort takes you back to page 1.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Your view lives in the link.** The search, filter, sort and page are all part of the page address. Reload and you land on the same list; copy the link and a friend sees exactly what you see, for example "every verified token, by market cap".
{% endhint %}

## Reading a token card

Each token is a card coloured with the project's own **brand colour**: a strip across the top and a ring around the logo. Tokens without a brand kit keep a neutral look. At a glance, a card shows:

* the token's **logo**, **name** and **ticker**, with a **verified** checkmark when the project has been checked,
* its **Price**, its change over the last **24h** (green when up, red when down) and its **Supply**,
* badges for what it offers: **Holders pool** when holders can stake it, **Reward programs** when a staking program is running for it, and **Not traded yet** when it isn't in any liquidity pool.

Click a card and the token's page opens **in a new tab**, so your place in the directory stays where you left it.

**For example.** You heard about a gaming guild's token at a meetup but forgot the exact name. You type "guild", spot **$GUILD** with its purple strip and the **Holders pool** badge, and open its page in a new tab. Later, curious what else is out there, you pick **Staking rewards** and **Sort: Market cap**, and send the link to a friend who wants the same list.
{% endhint %}
