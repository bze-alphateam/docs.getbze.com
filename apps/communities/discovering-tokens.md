# Discovering Tokens

The [Communities app](https://communities.getbze.com) opens on a directory of every community token — the tokens people have created with Token Factory. It is the front door to the projects building on BeeZee: browse them, find one that catches your eye, and step into its world.

## Browsing the directory

Tokens are laid out as a grid of cards, listed **alphabetically** and spread across pages, so you can page through the whole collection at your own pace. Each card gives you the essentials at a glance:

* the token's **logo**,
* its **name and ticker** (like $PAGE or $GUILD),
* a **verified** badge when the project has been checked, and
* the **total supply**.

Click any card to open that token's **dedicated page**, where everything the community offers lives — its staking programs, a way to get the token, its burn stats, and more. Each token page even rebrands the app around that project, so it feels less like a shared directory and more like the token's own site.

{% hint style="info" %}
**No keyword search yet.** For now, finding a token means browsing the alphabetical pages rather than typing its name into a search box. A search feature is something that may come later; until then, the pages are quick to flip through.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Only community tokens appear here.** The directory lists tokens created with Token Factory. BZE itself and coins bridged in from other networks are not part of this list — this is the home for projects built on BeeZee.
{% endhint %}
