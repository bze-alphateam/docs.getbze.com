# Overview

The CoinTrunk Extension is a self-custodial browser wallet for the BeeZee network. It safely holds BZE and the tokens created on BeeZee — you can receive coins and check your balances, with sending on the way — and your keys stay on your own device, with no account to sign up for.

What makes it stand out is that it adapts to the token you use: for a token that qualifies, the wallet takes on that token's name and look, so a community can give its members a wallet that feels entirely their own. By default, it wears its own CoinTrunk look.

{% hint style="info" %}
The CoinTrunk Extension is still in development. Availability and features will be announced as it gets closer to release.
{% endhint %}
